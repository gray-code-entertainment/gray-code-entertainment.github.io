HoodBoss

Boston’s Next Voice. Built from the streets, ready for the world.

Hoodboss is a Boston-based hip-hop artist with a unique sound, raw story-telling, and a relentless work ethic. Coming straight from the heart of the city, he delivers music that speaks on real life — the struggles, the wins, and the mindset it takes to survive and win. With a style that’s authentic, versatile, and undeniable, Hoodboss continues to build a loyal fanbase and make his mark in the streets and beyond. His journey is about more than just music; it’s about legacy. Hoodboss represents the hustle, the culture, and the city of Boston.
