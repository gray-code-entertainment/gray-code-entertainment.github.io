Size Guide

Size Guide

All measurements are in inches, with garments measured laid flat. Chest width is measured across the chest 1 inch below the armhole. Body length is measured from the high point of shoulder to the finished hem. Sleeve length is measured from center back.

Measurements may vary slightly. Chest width is measured flat — double it for the full chest circumference.

Each product's own page has its size chart and worn-model photo.

📦

Shipping

Each item is printed to order and shipped to your US address. Shipping is included in the price — no extra charge at checkout. Tee: about 1 day to print, then 2–5 business days to arrive. Hoodie: about 3 days to print, then 2–5 business days to arrive. Never Told Hoodie: printed to order, then 2–5 business days to arrive. Never Told Denim (jacket + jeans): international fulfillment, 10–17 days to arrive.
